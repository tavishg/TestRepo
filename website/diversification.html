Why Diversify?

The Case for Bitcoin in a Diversified Portfolio

Potential Benefits

  • ✓ Low Correlation: Bitcoin has historically shown low correlation with traditional assets, providing true diversification.
  • ✓ Asymmetric Upside: Small allocations (1-5%) can significantly boost returns if Bitcoin appreciates.
  • ✓ Inflation Hedge: Fixed supply makes it a potential hedge against monetary inflation.
  • ✓ Growing Adoption: Institutional adoption continues to increase, potentially reducing volatility over time.

Important Risks

  • ! Extreme Volatility: Bitcoin can lose 50%+ in months. Only invest what you can afford to lose.
  • ! Regulatory Risk: Government actions could impact value and accessibility.
  • ! No Cash Flows: Unlike stocks or bonds, Bitcoin generates no income—returns depend entirely on price appreciation.
  • ! Security Concerns: Self-custody requires technical knowledge; exchange risks exist.

Research Suggests

Studies from firms like Fidelity and Yale have found that a small Bitcoin allocation (1-5%) may improve risk-adjusted returns due to its low correlation with other assets. However, past performance doesn't guarantee future results, and Bitcoin remains highly speculative.

Diversification Principles

🎯

Correlation Matters

Assets that move independently provide better diversification than those that rise and fall together.

⚖️

Rebalance Regularly

Review your allocation annually and rebalance when it drifts more than 5% from your target.

🌍

Go Global

International stocks provide exposure to 50%+ of global market cap and reduce home country bias.

📊

Risk = Age

A common rule: subtract your age from 110 for your stock allocation. At 30, that's 80% stocks.
