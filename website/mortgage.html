Mortgage Tips for First-Time Buyers

🎯

28/36 Rule

Keep housing costs under 28% of gross income, and total debt under 36%. This keeps your finances healthy.

📊

Compare Rates

Even 0.25% lower interest can save tens of thousands over your loan. Get quotes from multiple lenders.

💰

Extra Payments

One extra payment per year can shave years off your mortgage and save thousands in interest.

📋

Hidden Costs

Budget for closing costs (2-5% of loan), maintenance (1% of home value/year), and potential HOA fees.
