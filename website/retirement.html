Building Your Retirement Income Portfolio

A well-structured retirement portfolio generates income from multiple sources while preserving capital. Here's a sample allocation:

Bonds 40%
Div. Stocks 30%
REITs 20%
Cash 10%
Bonds (Stability & Income)
Dividend Stocks (Growth & Income)
REITs (Real Estate Income)
Cash (Liquidity & Safety)

The Bucket Strategy

Consider dividing your retirement savings into three buckets: short-term (1-2 years of expenses in cash), medium-term (3-7 years in bonds), and long-term (remaining in stocks for growth). This provides stability while maintaining growth potential.

Keys to a Successful Retirement

🎯

Start Early

Time is your biggest asset. Starting at 25 vs 35 can mean twice the retirement savings.

📈

Maximize Matches

Always contribute enough to get your full employer 401(k) match. It's free money.

🛡️

Diversify Income

Don't rely on one source. Mix Social Security, savings, and possibly part-time income.

💊

Plan for Healthcare

Healthcare costs often surprise retirees. Budget for Medicare and supplemental insurance.
